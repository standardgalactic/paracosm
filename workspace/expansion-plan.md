# Expansion Plan — *Mathematical Proof for the Existence of God*

Status as of this note: **208 pages**, target **400**. No new appendices (stay at A–Z plus
"Open Problems, Continued") — new *sections* inside existing chapters and appendices
are fine. Every addition rigorous, cohesive, hand-verified before shipping; math-heavy
additions are welcome; ambiguities and unstated assumptions should be found and fixed,
not politely stepped around.

## 0. Top priority: Chapter 22 has a real, unresolved gap

While reviewing Chapter 22 to plan its expansion, found that **Proposition 22.4 only
proves existence** of the cofinal limit — it is literally titled `[Existence]` — but
**Appendix B tells the reader uniqueness was also established there**:

> "the work establishing existence and uniqueness (Proposition 22.4) is complete before
> the symbol $G$ is introduced at all"

This is false as the book currently stands. Worse, uniqueness is not obviously *true*
for the construction as written: Definition 22.1's "refinement chain" is general enough
that it is not obvious two different admissibility-improving chains from the same $W_0$
must converge to the same cofinal limit. If they don't, $G$ isn't well-defined by
Definition 22.6 without also fixing *which* chain — a real problem, not a stylistic one.

**This must be resolved before any decorative staircase-building happens**, and it
should probably drive the whole shape of the expanded chapter rather than be one lemma
among several. Two candidate resolutions, to be evaluated by actually working the proof:

- **(A) Tighten the definition.** Redefine $G$ as the cofinal limit of the *canonical*
  chain generated by repeated application of $T$ (Chapter 21) — $W_0, T(W_0), T(T(W_0)),
  \ldots$ — rather than of an arbitrary admissibility-improving refinement chain. Since
  $T$ is a deterministic function (Definition 21.1), this chain is uniquely determined
  by $W_0$ alone, and uniqueness becomes close to immediate. Cost: narrows Definition
  22.6 and requires checking that the canonical chain is itself always
  admissibility-improving with $\Delta_R \to 0$ whenever *any* such chain exists (may
  need its own lemma).
- **(B) Prove genuine chain-independence.** Show that *any* two admissibility-improving
  refinement chains from $W_0$ with $\Delta_R \to 0$ converge to the same $W_\infty$.
  Stronger and more satisfying if true, but needs to actually be checked — construct
  candidate counterexamples first (two different repair orderings from the same
  starting world) before assuming it holds.

Whichever resolution is correct, Appendix B's misdescription needs fixing regardless,
and this is the fix most worth doing first — it's a correctness issue, not a padding
question.

## 1. Chapter 22 staircase (once §0 is resolved)

Sketch only — exact lemma statements need to be checked against whichever resolution of
§0 is chosen, and against the *current* proof text at execution time, not assumed from
this note. Rough shape, following the reviewer's original suggested rhythm
(lemma → corollary → example → remark → lemma → proposition → theorem → corollary →
interpretation), adapted to what's actually true here:

1. **Lemma (pointwise stabilization).** The current proof of Proposition 22.4 *asserts*
   that local data stabilizes pointwise along the chain without actually deriving this
   from the refinement-chain definition — check whether Definition 22.1's "agreeing
   wherever both defined" clause is strong enough to force this on its own, or whether
   it depends on how $T$-repairs interact with already-defined overlap data (see §0).
   This should become a real, separately-proved lemma either way.
2. **Corollary.** $W_\infty$ is well-defined (Definition 22.3's eventual-constancy
   condition is met) — falls out of the lemma.
3. **Worked example.** Trace pointwise stabilization concretely through the existing
   toy chain (the $\{a,b,c\}$ world already used throughout Ch. 11–13, 21).
4. **Remark.** Honest comparison/contrast with Cauchy-sequence completion — where the
   analogy holds and where it doesn't (this construction stabilizes in finitely many
   steps at each point rather than merely getting arbitrarily close).
5. **Lemma.** $\Delta_R$ behaves well under the limit (the part of the current proof
   that invokes Proposition 20.3) — separate this out explicitly.
6. **Proposition.** $\Delta_R(W_\infty) = 0$ — clean statement, now genuinely following
   from the two lemmas rather than one dense paragraph.
7. **The uniqueness result from §0**, wherever it best fits — likely right after
   existence, before Theorem 22.5.
8. **Theorem 22.5 (No Interpretive Leap)** — currently fine, probably stays as is, maybe
   gets a one-line pointer back to the new uniqueness result.
9. **Interpretation / closing prose** — bridge to Definition 22.6, updated to correctly
   describe what's now actually been proven.

A small figure (level sets of $\Delta_R$ shrinking toward zero along the chain, or a
sequence of refining covers nested visually) would fit naturally here and hasn't been
built yet — candidate for the TikZ project too (see §4).

## 2. Plain-language chapter openings

New idea from this session: each numbered chapter (and probably the unnumbered bridge
chapters too) gets one additional paragraph, in plain language, with no math notation
and without using the words "chapter" or "section" — just stating directly what the
material is about and why it matters, the way you'd explain it out loud to someone who
doesn't read notation. Not a replacement for the existing technical opening paragraphs,
an *addition* alongside them.

None written yet. This is a large, mechanical-but-not-trivial task across all 28
numbered chapters plus the unnumbered ones — best done as its own pass rather than
folded into the exposition-expansion work, since it's a different kind of writing
(no verification/renumbering risk, since it's pure prose, but real craft to get the
tone right and avoid it reading as a summary/abstract rather than a piece of the book's
own voice).

Suggested order: do it chapter-by-chapter alongside whatever chapter is being
mathematically expanded that session, so the plain-language paragraph can be written
with full knowledge of the final content rather than needing revision later.

## 6. Standing rules, restated for future sessions

- No new appendices. Sections within existing ones are fine.
- Every addition hand-verified for actual correctness before shipping, not just
  citation numbering — this has caught genuine errors roughly ten times over the
  project's life and should never be skipped.
- Any new numbered environment inserted mid-chapter shifts everything after it —
  always run the full citation audit afterward and fix every downstream reference,
  across the whole book, not just the local area.
- Plain-language openings and pure-prose additions carry no renumbering risk and can
  be done quickly; new Lemmas/Propositions/Examples always trigger the renumbering
  fallout and should be batched thoughtfully within a session.
