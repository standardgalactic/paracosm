\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

Book 11's Chapter 9 previewed a tensor product on objects of $\mathbf{RSVPField}$, establishing only enough structure to note that RSVP's curvature algebra becomes a symmetric monoidal category. The present book completes the category-theoretic stage of the RSVP cosmology by constructing that monoidal category in full. Intelligence, across this book's twelve chapters, is defined as the monoidal closure of curvature, the capacity of systems to combine their entropy flows coherently without loss of smoothness. Thought becomes collaboration in this book precisely because association, the categorical operation this book develops, is what collaboration formally is.
